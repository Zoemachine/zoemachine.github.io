Why ZOE.

Keep your data, and outsource just the most intensive AI “thinking.”

01

Reduce AI Expenses.

Frontier AI costs will always be sensitive to compute (token) economics, and token counts naturally balloon as AI gets “smarter.” ZOE AI uses your laptop hardware to “preprocess” local data (like a junior associate) so that just the more difficult issues (typically done by a partner) are tackled by the hyperscaler, reducing compute costs by up to 99%.

02

Protect Client Data.

Sophisticated clients are becoming more cautious about third-party AI vendors and datacenter security. ZOE AI keeps the vast majority of the client's data (including all client PII) local, on your system of choice. A ZOE AI user can calmly tell their client that “your documents never leave my laptop” while they leverage all of the benefits of modern legal tech powered by the world's best hyperscalers.

03

Protect Firm Data.

In today's AI driven world, your templates, preferred clauses, and negotiating playbooks represent the differentiators and competitive “moat” of your firm. When using ZOE AI, these precious assets that your firm has built over decades will stay on your laptop, desktop or server of choice. When you search for a preferred template or legal clause, the search is entirely local; when the hyperscaler (of your choice) helps you draft, the drafting flow sends just the skeleton it needs, about 90% fewer tokens, and sees small snippets of information on a need-to-know basis, when you allow it.

How does it work

What stays on the laptop

Everything that matters stays here: your clients' documents and the whole data room; your firm's clause library, precedents and templates, which are the work product the firm has built over decades and the competitive moat that sets it apart; and every matter's working files, with every confidential name, number and address ZOE marks in them.

Point ZOE at your precedents, whether ten of them or fifty thousand, and when you ask for a strong environmental rep you get your firm's best one, from the deal it actually closed in.

None of it leaves the laptop.

What happens with no internet

ZOE is air-gapped by default. Due diligence, the record of who signed and in what capacity, clause and template search, and drafting in Word all keep running with the connection off, which effectively creates an air-gap between your client's documents and the outside world. It works on a plane, in a data room with guest wifi you would never put client documents on, and in a courthouse basement with no signal at all.
